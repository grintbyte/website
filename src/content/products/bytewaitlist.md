When a product runs out of stock, most interested customers leave and forget to come back. ByteWaitlist catches that interest before it walks away: an out-of-stock product shows a waitlist button instead of a dead end, and when you restock, everyone waiting gets an email inviting them to complete the purchase.

## How it works

1. **A product sells out.** Its page automatically shows a waitlist button in place of the unavailable purchase.
2. **Customers register interest** with one click, and can review their waitlisted products any time from their own account page.
3. **You see the demand** in the admin dashboard: which products people are waiting for, and how many.
4. **You restock.** ByteWaitlist emails everyone on that product's waitlist, turning the restock into a sales event instead of a quiet inventory update.

## Demand data you did not have before

Waitlists double as a demand signal: products with long waitlists tell you what to reorder first and how much, before you spend anything on promotion.

## Who it is for

Stores selling limited-stock or fast-moving products, retailers who restock regularly, and any merchant tired of watching out-of-stock pages send ready-to-buy customers to a competitor. A built-in how-to guide covers setup, and the developer's email and Discord are in the settings when you need help.
