Static product photos show a handful of angles and one configuration. Byte3DView replaces them with an interactive 3D model: customers rotate the product freely, switch colors, materials and finishes, and watch the product change in real time while they decide.

## Explore every angle

Full 360-degree rotation lets shoppers inspect shape, design and detail the way they would in a showroom, instead of trusting three photos and a swatch chart.

## Customize every detail

- **Colors**: pick a color and see it applied to the model instantly.
- **Materials and textures**: switch a sofa's upholstery between leather, fabric and other available materials, then try each one in different finishes.
- **Configurable components**: products with multiple options become explorable combinations rather than a dropdown of guesses.

## Free and Pro

The free version delivers the spin: 360-degree rotation on the product page, interactive swipe, click or drag control, plus the admin upload and viewer settings to set it up. Pro turns the viewer into a configurator with real-time color customization, material and texture switching, configurable variations, and the admin tools to upload and map an image sequence to every option. See the comparison below for the exact split.

## Made for customizable goods

Byte3DView earns its keep on products where configuration drives the sale: furniture, sofas and chairs, interior and home furnishing products, and any catalog offering multiple materials, colors or finishes. A customer who has rotated, recolored and configured a product knows exactly what they are ordering, which means more confident purchases and fewer surprises at delivery.
