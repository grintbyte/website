BytePopup is a lightweight popup manager for WordPress. It gives website owners one admin screen to create, customize and organize every popup on the site: announcements, promos, lead capture, or anything a page needs to say before a visitor leaves.

## What it does

Each popup is a simple form: give it a name, choose where it shows, write the content in the familiar WordPress editor, and pick how it appears and disappears. Show it on scroll, after a time delay, or on a repeating interval; close it on mouse leave or on click. Position it with presets from center to any corner, or place it precisely with custom X,Y coordinates. Every popup can link to a page or post on your site.

## Templates that save the next ten minutes

The Pro template library ships ready-made layouts for common use cases, and any popup you design can be saved, cloned and reused as a template of your own. Set up a campaign once, reuse it every season.

## Who it is for

Website owners, store operators, marketers and admins who want popups managed in one place, without a page builder dependency or a plugin that needs its own onboarding. The built-in how-to guide covers every screen, and the developer's email and Discord are right in the settings when you need a human.

The free version on WordPress.org covers the essentials: popup management, the core triggers and two positions. Pro adds the template library, template editor, interval triggers and full position control. See the comparison below for the exact split.
