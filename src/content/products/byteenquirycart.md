ByteEnquiryCart is a flexible ordering plugin for online stores that need more than one way to sell. It lets a single store offer two purchasing methods at once: customers can request a quotation for selected products, or buy fixed-price products directly through the regular cart and checkout.

## Two ordering methods, one store

Not every product can or should be sold through a standard checkout. Some need custom pricing, bulk quotations, negotiation or a conversation first. With ByteEnquiryCart you decide per product:

- **Enquiry-based ordering**: the product joins an enquiry list instead of the cart, and the customer submits a quotation request for everything on it.
- **Direct purchasing**: the product shows its price and a normal Add to Cart button, and checkout works exactly as the store always has.

## The enquiry list

Customers get a dedicated place to collect the products they are interested in and send one quotation request for all of them, without touching the checkout. Your sales team picks up the conversation from there.

## Who it is for

B2B and wholesale businesses, online retailers, manufacturers and distributors, made-to-order shops, and any store that mixes fixed-price products with products that deserve a quote first.

## Built to grow with your stack

ByteEnquiryCart is designed platform-independent at its core, so the same flexible-ordering model can expand to other commerce ecosystems through dedicated integrations.
