ByteShipper gives store owners real control over shipping costs. Instead of forcing every product through a single store-wide rate, it lets you configure shipping per product, per destination, and per rule type, then maintain it all without editing products one by one.

## Rates that match how you actually ship

- **Per-product rates**: every product can carry its own shipping cost, for catalogs where a sticker and a wardrobe should never cost the same to ship.
- **Domestic and international rules**: set one rate for your home country and different rates for overseas orders, so cross-border shipping stops eating your margin.
- **Percentage-based fees**: calculate shipping as a percentage of price where that fits the product.
- **Fixed amounts**: predictable flat charges where customers expect them.

## Built for large catalogs

Bulk management applies rate changes to many products at once, keeping rules consistent across hundreds of items, while individual configuration stays available for the products that need an exception.

## Who it is for

Online stores shipping products with genuinely different costs, retailers selling both domestically and internationally, and anyone maintaining a catalog too large to update by hand.
