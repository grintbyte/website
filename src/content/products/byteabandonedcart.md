Not every customer who fills a cart finishes the purchase. Some get distracted, some postpone, some just leave. ByteAbandonedCart watches for those moments and turns them into second chances: it monitors abandoned carts, tells the store owner when they happen, and reminds the customer to come back and complete checkout.

## Monitor. Notify. Remind. Recover.

- **Monitoring**: every cart that stalls before checkout becomes visible instead of silently disappearing.
- **Owner notifications**: you hear about incomplete purchases as they happen, so follow-up stops depending on luck.
- **Customer reminders**: automated notifications bring the unfinished order back to the customer's attention at the right time.
- **Recovery**: a shopper who already picked products is the warmest lead a store has. Reminders give that intent somewhere to go.

## Who it is for

E-commerce stores and online retailers of any size: anyone watching traffic arrive, carts fill, and a chunk of checkouts never complete. ByteAbandonedCart makes better use of the traffic you already have before you spend on more.

## Less manual chasing

Without tooling, recovering carts means digging through order logs and guessing. ByteAbandonedCart puts monitoring, notifications and customer follow-up in one place, so recovery becomes routine instead of a project.
